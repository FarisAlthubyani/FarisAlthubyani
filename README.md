## 📊 Featured Projects

### 🛒 [Kaleem Store Sales Analysis](https://github.com/FarisAlthubyani/kaleem-sales-analysis)
**Real data from my own Salla store (Jan – Sep 2026)**

<img src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/785b0a6d-1b61-4cae-9218-cf58dea8ce16" alt="Kaleem Sales Dashboard" width="100%">

- Analyzed **275 orders** worth **18,377 SAR** across products, cities, time of day, gender and traffic sources.
- Found that the first TikTok campaign lifted monthly sales from **~1.2K to ~4.6K SAR**, while ROAS dropped from **3.55 to 1.96**, a sign of ad fatigue.
- TikTok brought **96% of traffic** but **40% of sales**; direct visits brought **4% of traffic** and **60% of sales**.
- Turned the findings into clear recommendations for ads, targeting and channel tracking.

`Excel` `Power Query` `Power BI` `DAX` `Data Modeling`

### 🚀 [Mission Control: Rocket Flight Prediction](https://github.com/FarisAlthubyani/rocket-flight-prediction)
**Prediction Challenge, College of Computing Club**

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/FarisAlthubyani/rocket-flight-prediction/main/dashboard.png" alt="Mission Control Dashboard" width="100%">

- Profiled **50 historical flights** and compared averages and medians across Success, At Risk and Failure.
- Built decision rules from the data: one weak factor means At Risk, two or more mean Failure.
- Predicted the outcome of **15 new flights** and explained every prediction.
- Designed a one-page Power BI dashboard with KPIs, a risk scatter plot and a flight log.

`Excel` `Power BI` `Power Query` `Statistical Analysis`
